--json prints only paths. An agent iterating on quality needs, without a browser: section heading count and list, outline entry count, theme names with concept counts, segments in effect with spans, speaker label distribution, frame count kept/captured, and the top proper-noun spelling variants (Omarchy/Omachi/Omarchi/Amache surfaced only by a hand-written scan). Add --report (text and --json) that prints exactly that from the final item, so the loop is: run, read the report, adjust flags, rerun. The spelling-variant list is what makes --key-term choosable without reading 5 hours of transcript.
