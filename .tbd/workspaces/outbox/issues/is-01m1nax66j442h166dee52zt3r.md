Concepts, outline, and synopsis each send the whole transcript in one call, which caps analysis quality long before it caps context. MAX_CONCEPTS=24 maps a 4-minute sketch well and a 5-hour conversation poorly. Extraction moves to time-based chunks (~30-60 min) snapped to section boundaries, with results stitched.

## Notes

MEASURED on Lex #501 (5.26 h), confirming the cap is the binding constraint:

  24 concepts / 5.26 h = 4.6 per hour.
  For comparison the 22-min SNL example gets the same 24, i.e. ~65 per hour.
  So coverage per hour is ~14x worse on the long recording purely because MAX_CONCEPTS
  is an absolute cap applied to a whole-document call.

Whole-document extraction is not hitting a context limit at this length (the doc is ~74k
tokens; add_transcript_outline ran in 67 s and add_transcript_description in 13 s). The
cap is a deliberate output ceiling, and it is the ceiling that fails — one call cannot
name 150 concepts well even if it fits.

Chunk sizing against the measured section list:
  194 sections over 5.26 h. ~30-min chunks -> 11 chunks of ~18 sections;
  ~60-min chunks -> 5 chunks of ~39 sections.
  At 24 concepts per chunk that is 264 (30-min) or 120 (60-min) before the merge dedupes,
  which is the right order of magnitude for a five-hour conversation.
